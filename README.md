### 🎨 About Me

Trained in Dentistry, I found that my passion for problem-solving and attention to detail translates seamlessly into technology. I am a tech enthusiast focused on building efficient, user-friendly digital experiences while constantly evolving my **Full-Stack** skills.

* 🧠 **Mindset:** I love embracing change and thrive on challenges. I bring an analytical approach to code, ensuring that every detail counts.
* 🌍 **Perspective:** Travel and exploring new cultures have made me highly adaptable, observant, and open to diverse ideas.
